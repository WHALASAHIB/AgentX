FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Agent X is an autonomous AI-powered trading platform. You connect your MT5 accounts (FTMO, demo, or live), configure risk parameters and strategy preferences, and deploy AI agents that execute trades 24/7. Agents analyze market conditions, manage position sizing, and stop out of trades per your risk rules — no manual intervention required.
Agent X supports any MetaTrader 5 account — FTMO challenge & verification accounts, demo accounts from any broker (MetaQuotes-Demo, FTMO-Demo, IC Markets, etc.), and live brokerage accounts. The Bridge connects securely via the MT5 API and can manage up to 5+ accounts simultaneously on paid plans.
Risk management is enforced at the infrastructure level — not just recommended. You set a max risk per trade (default 1% of balance), daily drawdown limits, and daily trade caps. The agent physically respects these limits. Our Self-Healing Watchdog monitors everything 24/7 and restarts any failed components automatically. No strategy can override your risk parameters.
Zero coding required. The entire setup is configuration-based through the dashboard. You pick your account, set your risk numbers (percentage, stop-loss, trade limits), and the agent handles everything. Advanced users can customize strategies, but the default configuration is production-ready out of the box.
Every trade follows your predefined risk rules — the agent will close the trade at SL if it moves against you, and the per-trade risk cap ensures no single trade can damage your account. After a loss, the agent adjusts position sizing on the next trade automatically. Our backtesting engine lets you validate strategies on 300+ real bars before going live so you know the risk profile upfront.
