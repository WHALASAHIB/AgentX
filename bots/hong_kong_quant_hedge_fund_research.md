# Starting a Quantitative Hedge Fund in Hong Kong - Comprehensive Research

## 1. SFC Licensing Requirements: Type 9 (Asset Management)

A quantitative hedge fund managing third-party capital needs a Type 9 (Asset Management) license under the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO).

### Key Requirements
- Incorporation: Must be a Hong Kong-incorporated company
- Responsible Officers (ROs): Minimum 2 ROs per regulated activity
- Fit and Proper Test: Financial status, education, experience, reputation
- Competence: Proper business structure, internal controls, qualified personnel

### Minimum Capital Requirements (from SFC)

Type 9 with 'not hold client assets' condition:
No minimum paid-up share capital, minimum liquid capital of HK$100,000 (~$12,800 USD)

Type 9 holding client assets:
Min paid-up share capital of HK$5,000,000, min liquid capital of HK$3,000,000

### Annual Licensing Fees
- Licensed corporation (per RA): HK$4,740
- Responsible Officer: HK$4,740
- Licensed Representative (non-RO): HK$1,790

### Ongoing Obligations
- Semi-annual financial resources returns
- Annual audited accounts within 4 months of FYE
- Annual return within 1 month of license anniversary
- CPT: 12 hours/year for ROs, 10 hours/year for LRs
- 7 business day notification for changes
- AML compliance program required

### Personnel Requirements
- At least 2 Responsible Officers approved by SFC for Type 9
- Each RO must pass SFC Licensing Examination (HKSI papers)
- Relevant industry experience (typically 3-5+ years minimum)

## 2. Legal Structure

### Typical Structure
- Fund vehicle: Cayman Islands Exempted Limited Partnership (most common)
- Manager: Hong Kong-incorporated company with SFC Type 9 license
- Prime broker: Major bank (custodian, execution, financing)

### Structure Options
- Cayman ELP - Most common; tax-neutral; familiar to investors
- Cayman SPC - Allows multiple sub-funds
- HK Open-Ended Fund Company (OFC) - HK-domiciled
- HK Unit Trust - Traditional structure

### Key Regulatory Notes
- SFC regulates the manager, not the fund itself (if privately placed)
- Private placement to professional investors only (HK$8M+ portfolio for individuals)
- No SFC authorization of fund required for professional investor offerings

## 5. AUM Needed to Be Viable

Standard 2-and-20 model (2% management fee + 20% performance fee).

- At HK$600K annual costs -> need HK$30M AUM at pure 2% fee
- At HK$1M costs -> need HK$50M AUM at pure 2% fee
- With 10% returns + 20% perf fee, AUM needs drop by ~50%
- Minimum viable AUM: ~HK$30M ($3.8M USD)
- Reality: Most institutions want $50M+ AUM

## 6. Single-Founder Algo Trading - Challenges

- Two Responsible Officer rule is a hard blocker without a second person
- FTMO prop firm track record likely not counted by SFC as managed assets
- Single-person shop faces added scrutiny on compliance/operations
- Institutional investors see key-person risk
- Raising $5M+ as unknown single founder is extremely difficult
- Prime broker may require minimum AUM for onboarding

## 7. Prop Firm vs Hedge Fund Trade-offs

### Prop Trading Firm
- Capital: Own/firm capital
- Regulation: Low to none
- Costs: Low
- Revenue: 100% of profits
- Scalability: Limited by own capital
- Risk: Full downside on own capital
- Launch time: Days
- Single-founder viable: Yes

### Hedge Fund (Type 9 Licensed)
- Capital: Third-party investor capital
- Regulation: High (SFC license, ongoing compliance)
- Costs: High (legal, compliance, audit, fund structure)
- Revenue: 2% management fee + 20% performance fee
- Scalability: Unlimited (can raise from investors)
- Risk: Investors bear fund losses
- Launch time: 6-9 months
- Single-founder viable: Very difficult
