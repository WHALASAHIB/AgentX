# Research Council Findings — Trading Bot "Millions in Weeks" Claims

## Date
18 June 2026

## Source
Research & Invocation Division Council Investigation (4 parallel agents)

## Executive Summary
**Every viral claim of a trading bot turning $500 into $1M in weeks is fake.** 
No verified or audited track record exists for any such claim. The mathematics make it 
impossible at the shorter timeframes, and the cost structure of trading makes small accounts 
(<$5K) have negative expected value regardless of strategy.

## The Small Account Problem

On accounts under $5,000:
- Spread + commission per trade: ~$12 (gold/forex round-turn)
- 2% risk rule on $500 = max $10 risk per trade
- **Risk budget is SMALLER than trading cost**
- Effective R:R drops from 3:1 to ~1.5:1 after costs
- Real minimum for edge to survive: **~$5,000**
