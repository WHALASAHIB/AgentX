# Q4 2025 Earnings Report — Nvidia Corporation (NVDA)

## Executive Summary

Nvidia reported record revenue of $39.3 billion for Q4 2025, up 78% year-over-year. Data Center revenue was $35.6 billion, driven by continued demand for Hopper GPU architecture and early ramp of Blackwell systems. Gaming revenue was $2.5 billion, up 15% YoY.

## Revenue Breakdown

### Data Center
- Revenue: $35.6B (+82% YoY)
- Key drivers: AI training/inference workloads, enterprise adoption
- Major customers: Cloud service providers (45% of DC revenue), enterprise (30%), consumer internet (25%)

### Gaming
- Revenue: $2.5B (+15% YoY)
- GeForce RTX 50 series launch driving upgrade cycle
- Gaming laptop segment grew 22% YoY

### Professional Visualization
- Revenue: $0.6B (+35% YoY)
- Omniverse enterprise adoption growing

### Automotive
- Revenue: $0.5B (+40% YoY)
- DRIVE Orin and Thor platforms

## Margin Analysis

### Gross Margin
- GAAP: 73.5% (expanding 220bps YoY)
- Non-GAAP: 75.8% (expanding 180bps YoY)
- Improvement driven by mix shift to Data Center and cost optimization

### Operating Margin
- GAAP: 62.1%
- Non-GAAP: 64.3%
- R&D spending: $4.2B (10.7% of revenue)
- SG&A: $1.8B (4.6% of revenue)

## Balance Sheet Highlights

- Cash & equivalents: $42.5B
- Free cash flow: $18.2B (FCF margin: 46.3%)
- Share buybacks: $8.5B in Q4
- Dividends: $0.21 per share quarterly

## Forward Guidance

### Q1 2026 Outlook
- Revenue guidance: $42.0B ± 2%
- GAAP gross margin: ~74.0%
- Operating expenses: ~$6.5B
- Effective tax rate: ~14.5%

### Key Strategic Initiatives
1. Blackwell Ultra ramp in H2 2026
2. Rubin architecture development on track
3. AI Enterprise software platform expansion
4. Sovereign AI infrastructure buildouts
5. Autonomous vehicle pipeline expansion

## Risk Factors

1. Export controls and geopolitical tensions affecting China revenue (~15% of total)
2. Supply chain constraints for advanced packaging (CoWoS)
3. Hyperscaler in-sourcing of custom AI chips (TPU, Trainium)
4. Regulatory scrutiny of AI markets in US and EU
5. Cyclical semiconductor downturn risk in gaming segment
